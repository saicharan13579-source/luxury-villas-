The courtyard heart

Rooms are arranged around a central void that brings daylight and cross-ventilation deep into the plan.

Double-height volumes

The living space rises through two levels, connecting family floors visually and filling the home with light.

Layered privacy

Spaces move from public to family to private as you go deeper and higher into the villa.

Indoor–outdoor flow

Every key room opens onto a deck, verandah, garden or terrace — the landscape becomes part of the house.
